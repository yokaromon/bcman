# Detect cards locally from rotated rectangles

Card Detection derives every candidate from local image contours as rotated rectangles or four-point approximations, and no longer asks the image AI for corner coordinates. This supersedes the image-AI detection fallback of ADR 0006 and the fractional-coordinate contract of ADR 0009: even with correctly scaled coordinates, the model's corner estimates were too imprecise to crop from, and their overlap with local candidates was too weak to deduplicate, so one physical card became several Business Cards with garbled crops. Candidates are filtered by card-like proportion and size, collapsed when one is largely contained in another, capped as a cost safety valve, and ordered top-left first; corners are normalized to a clockwise order before perspective correction. A Photo with no candidate falls back to treating the whole Photo as one Business Card at low confidence rather than ending in Detection Failure.
